Add to Home Screen

Put this app on your phone’s home screen so it opens like a normal app, with its own icon.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

On iPhone this works only in Safari, not inside Facebook, Messenger or other in-app browsers.

Note: this only works when the app is opened from a web address (https://…). If you opened the file from your phone’s storage, upload it to a free web host (for example GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages) and open that address instead.

Writing help from ChatGPT

This app has no built-in AI. ChatGPT is a separate website, free to open. Here is how to use it for your memo:

  1. Write or paste your draft in the body text box first.
  2. Choose what you need:
  3. Click Copy & open ChatGPT. Your request and your text are copied, and chatgpt.com opens in a new tab.
  4. In ChatGPT, click the message box, press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac), then Enter.
  5. Copy ChatGPT’s answer, come back here and paste it into the body text box.

Be careful: memorandums may be CONFIDENTIAL. ChatGPT is a public service, so remove names of victims, witnesses, suspects, case numbers and other sensitive details before pasting, and follow your unit’s rules on using AI tools.
